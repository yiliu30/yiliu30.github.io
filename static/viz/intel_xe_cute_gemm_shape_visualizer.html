7 · Concrete BF16 example

Why 256 × 256 × 32 works

M dimension

256 = 8 × 4 × 8

SG_M=8, each subgroup gets 32 rows, then 32/Atom_M(8)=4 DPAS positions.

N dimension

256 = 4 × 4 × 16

SG_N=4, each subgroup gets 64 columns, then 64/Atom_N(16)=4 DPAS positions.

K dimension

32 = 2 × 16

BF16 DPAS has Atom_K=16; a work-group K tile of 32 therefore contains 2 DPAS-depth steps.
