Important: these names span multiple Intel metric generations. Arc/ACM, VTune/Ponte Vecchio, and Lunar Lake/Xe2 do not always use identical semantics. If your counters come from Cobalt/XeSim, its target-specific definition wins.
Modern XVE metric documentation explicitly notes that multiple stall reasons may qualify in the same cycle; do not automatically sum percentages as mutually exclusive slices.
Fast mental model · audited
ALUWR / SBID

Both are dependency-side waits, but different dependencies: ALUWR is waiting for an ALU write/result; SBID is scoreboard token/dependency state. Neither alone proves DRAM latency or physical write-port contention.

PIPESTALL

The instruction can be ready yet unable to enter the target pipe. Intel VTune gives a GRF bank conflict as a concrete example.

SENDWR

This one must be source-scoped: Arc/ACM = waiting for SEND to write GRF; Lunar Lake-style metric = waiting for SEND dispatch from XVE.
